## Description

<!-- SECTION:DESCRIPTION:BEGIN -->
In the Killing Floor 1 Mountain Pass level (generated by scripts/kf1_mountain_pass.py, loaded from .tools/kf1/kf1-mountain-pass.mblevel.json with the kf1 pack installed), the shrine spot at roughly [93.8, 9.2, -188.5] sits under a corrugated roof. A player teleported to y 14.9 at that x/z fell to y 2.16 instead of landing at y 9.2, so the surface the converter treated as the floor at 9.2 held nobody in game; the shrine may be floating on brush collision the bundle does not give the player. Evidence frame: .tools/evidence/ac3-shrine.png (the camera is under the roof). Other shrines checked behave: the shrine at [67.5, 16.6, 176.2] charges when approached. The goal is that every shrine, chest and power-up anchor the converter writes rests on a surface the player can stand on and reach, and that the converter reports an anchor it cannot ground instead of floating it. Related context: the converter already logs anchors it skips for having no floor; docs/kf1-mountain-pass.md lists the open brush-collision questions (TASK-086.03.01).
