## Your next level starts here.

This is a **development project**, not a Steam Workshop subscription. Public installer downloads aren’t available yet. The guides explain how to get a package or build from source, install the mod, and make your first edit.

1. **Get set up.** You’ll need your own Steam copy of Megabonk and the mod, loaded through <a href="https://melon-loader.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">MelonLoader</a>.
2. **Open Bramblecrown Hill.** Once installed, choose it on the game’s Map Selection screen.
3. **Make one small change.** Press F8, move a rock, save as `my-bramblecrown`, and play your copy.
