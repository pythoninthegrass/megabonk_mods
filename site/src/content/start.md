## Your next level starts here.

Get **Megabonk Level Editor** on Thunderstore, then follow the player guide to install the mod and make your first edit. This is an unofficial mod, not a Steam Workshop subscription.

1. **Get set up.** You’ll need your own Steam copy of Megabonk and the mod, loaded through <a href="https://thunderstore.io/c/megabonk/p/BepInEx/BepInExPack_IL2CPP/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">BepInEx 6 IL2CPP</a>.
2. **Add a level.** The Thunderstore package does not include levels. Copy Bramblecrown Hill’s separately supplied `.mblevel.json` file into `UserData/Levels/`, then choose it on Map Selection.
3. **Make one small change.** Press F8, move a rock, save as `my-bramblecrown`, and play your copy.
