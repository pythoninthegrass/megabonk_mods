## Start with a hill. End somewhere new.

**Bramblecrown Hill** is an original example level, supplied separately from the editor package. A spiral climb links a forest meadow, graveyard gully, dungeon ruins, desert highland and a hell district—all built from the game’s own props.

Move your first rock, save a copy, and take it for a run. This starter needs no extra asset pack or custom soundtrack.

Your level lives in a **`.mblevel.json`** file. Share it like any other file; players put it in `UserData/Levels/` and find it beside the built-in maps. Levels using extra packs need those packs too, copied into `UserData/LevelPacks/`; the <a href="https://github.com/pythoninthegrass/megabonk_mods/blob/main/docs/player-guide.md#installing-an-asset-pack" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">player guide</a> shows the layout.
