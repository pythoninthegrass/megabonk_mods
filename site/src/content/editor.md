## Less imagining. More making.

Press **F8**. The run pauses, the camera flies free, and the level becomes your workbench. Shape the ground. Place a ruin. Move that rock just a little to the left.

Press **F8** again and you’re playing the same run, with your changes already there. No separate playtest build. No lost undo history.

- Raise, lower, slope and remove terrain on tile-based maps.
- Browse props from the game’s loaded maps and installed asset packs.
- Place, rotate and move objects. Undo, redo, save, repeat.

Terrain tools work on tile-based maps such as Forest and Graveyard. Desert and Hell support object editing, not terrain sculpting.
